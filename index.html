How RoofSquares works

Measure. Enter the footprint (plan) area and pick the pitch — RoofSquares multiplies by the standard slope factor to get the true roof area. Already measured the sloped surface (satellite report, ladder measurement)? Switch to “I measured the roof area” and enter it directly; pitch is then used only for the labor adder.

Squares & materials. Waste % (set by roof complexity, adjustable) is added for ordering: bundles, starter, underlayment rolls, ice & water shield for eaves + valleys, drip edge for eaves + rakes, ridge cap, nails and vents are calculated from your linear measurements. Every coverage number and price lives in Settings — localize them to your supplier.

True cost. Materials + install labor (per square, with pitch adders) + tear-off labor (per square per layer) + dump loads + permit + overhead = your true cost. Break-even is that number. Recommended prices to your target margin. Premium adds your premium markup.

Profit floor. Your floor is a minimum margin (default 25%) on the final price. Type a final price below the floor price and RoofSquares warns you in red and won’t save the bid unless you tick the one-time override and own the decision. This is the guardrail against winning roofs that lose money.

Defaults are planning numbers, not gospel. The pitch multipliers are standard geometry. Everything else — production rates, prices, coverage, the installed $/square band ($452–$1,208 national planning band) — is a starting default. Set your real supplier prices and crew rates in Settings before trusting a bid.

Your data. Everything is stored only in this browser (localStorage) on this device — no account, no cloud, no tracking, works fully offline. Use Export backup in Saved Bids before switching browsers or devices. Clearing browser data erases the app — export first.
